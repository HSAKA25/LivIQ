Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about how LIVIQ works and how this interactive browser demo handles your data.

1. What is LIVIQ?

LIVIQ is a smart household management platform concept designed to help households organize pantry and fridge inventory, track grocery spending, manage shopping lists, plan meals from available ingredients, and reduce avoidable food waste.

2. How does household inventory tracking work?

You can log groceries and household essentials with their category, quantity, unit, and expiry date. LIVIQ automatically highlights items that are running low in stock or approaching their expiration date so you can either restock or cook with them promptly.

3. Can LIVIQ help reduce food waste?

Yes. By surfacing items nearing expiry and matching your current pantry inventory to practical recipes, LIVIQ helps you prioritize perishable ingredients before they spoil and prevents overbuying items you already have in stock.

4. Does LIVIQ generate recipes using available ingredients?

In this interactive single-file demo, recipe suggestions use transparent, rule-based JavaScript matching that compares curated recipes against the ingredients currently in your demo inventory. A full production deployment would connect to a backend AI service to generate custom recipes dynamically.

5. Does LIVIQ require an account?

No account or sign-up is required to use this interactive website demo. In a full production SaaS environment, secure authentication and backend services would be required to support personal accounts and multi-user household sharing.

6. Is my demo data stored online?

No. This single-file application stores all demo changes locally in your browser using localStorage. It does not transmit your entries to any external server, cloud database, or third-party API, and data does not sync across different browsers or devices. You can clear and reset your local demo data at any time using the "Reset Demo" button.
