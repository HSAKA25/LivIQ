Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about LIVIQ and how this single-file interactive platform works.

1. What is LIVIQ?

LIVIQ is an intelligent household management platform concept designed to help households organize pantry and fridge inventory, track grocery spending, manage shopping lists, discover recipes from available ingredients, and reduce avoidable food waste.

2. How does household inventory tracking work?

You can log groceries and household essentials with their category, quantity, unit, purchase date, and expiry date. LIVIQ automatically flags items that are running low in stock or approaching their expiration date so you can cook with them promptly or restock.

3. Can LIVIQ help reduce food waste?

Yes. By surfacing items nearing expiry and pairing your current inventory with practical recipe suggestions, LIVIQ helps you cook perishable ingredients before they spoil and prevents overbuying items you already own.

4. Does LIVIQ generate recipes using available ingredients?

In this single-file demo, recipe suggestions use transparent, rule-based JavaScript matching that compares curated recipes against the ingredients currently in your demo inventory. A full cloud SaaS deployment would connect to backend generative AI models.

5. Does LIVIQ require an account?

No account or registration is required to use this interactive demo. In a future production environment, secure authentication and cloud database services would support multi-user household syncing.

6. Is my demo data stored online?

No. All demo data is stored exclusively in your browser using localStorage. No entries are sent to any remote server or database. You can reset to clean sample data anytime using the "Reset Demo" button.
